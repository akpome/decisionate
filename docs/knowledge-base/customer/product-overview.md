# What Decisionate does

Decisionate brings selected evidence from business datasets together so a team
can understand changes, choose an action, record its result and retain the
lesson. Compare metrics compares activity in one system with outcomes in
another. Business impact analysis estimates the impact of a recorded action using an
unaffected comparison and before-and-after history, when the evidence passes
the required checks. Correlation alone does not establish cause and effect;
an impact estimate depends on the comparison and stated business assumptions.

## Choose the right page

Use these sidebar links in the active workspace. Some are available only to
workspace owners or approved agency owners.

- [Dashboard](/dashboard): Open the current view. Choose a dataset and date
  range. Use Filters for the date or dimension column. In General Business
  Overview, Edit dashboard lets you choose Select Metrics or Compare Groups.
- [Dashboards](/dashboards): Choose a different view with Use Dashboard, then
  open it. Use Shared links here to manage dashboard links sent by email.
- [Decisions](/decisions): Open an action to review its evidence, targets and
  outcome, or choose Create decision to record a new action and review date.
- [Action Needed](/action-needed): Open items that need attention, such as a
  review due or results ready. Complete the follow-up in the linked decision.
- [Business Analysis](/business-analysis): Choose Compare metrics, Business
  impact or one of the other analysis tabs. Set up the analysis, then preview
  it before saving or creating a decision.
- [Budget Planner](/budget-planner): Choose the result to improve and separate
  spending options. Enter a budget and preview a possible future mix. It does
  not change real spending.
- [Weekly Reports](/weekly-reports): Pick what to track in Report setup,
  preview the last completed week, then choose Save changes. Open Scheduled
  email inside Weekly Reports to manage its delivery.
- [Relationships](/relationships): Choose two datasets and one metric from each.
  Preview the relationship, check the direction and delay, then save it.
- [Forecasts](/forecasts): Choose a dataset, metric and history range. Check the
  predicted values and warnings before using them to plan.
- [Datasets](/datasets): Choose Add data to upload a file or import from a
  supported link. Open a dataset row to inspect its dates, columns and metrics.
- [Entity Matching](/entity-matching): Choose Customers or Products and the
  fields that identify the same person or product across sources. Preview
  matches, check uncertain links, then save a separate linked dataset.
- [Connections](/connections): Choose Add under Connection sources. Finish
  account setup in Configure added connections, then sync or set a schedule.
- [Settings](/settings): Manage the active workspace. Depending on access,
  use Workspace Members, branding or hosted portal settings, client workspace
  tools or Delete workspace.
- [Billing](/billing): Review the plan and status for the active workspace,
  manage an existing subscription or choose a plan. Payment controls depend
  on who manages the workspace's subscription.
- [Help & Support](/help): Open Workflow guides to read the instructions for
  any tool, ask a product question, or open the support form for account help.

Business Analysis was also called Analysis and Instant analysis.
Business impact is a tool within Business Analysis. Its former standalone URL
redirects to that tool. It was previously labeled Assess a decision.
Business Analysis appears under the Analysis sidebar group; the group name is unchanged.
Weekly Reports replaces Weekly Review and Weekly Business Review in navigation.
Compare metrics is the trend and correlation tool; Business impact analysis is the
business-impact workflow. Existing analysis/report URLs and saved snapshots
remain valid; these names do not merge the two workflows or their settings.

## Where Insights went

Insights is no longer a separate analysis destination. Old Insights links
redirect to Business Analysis. The dashboard Analysis tab and its panels were removed;
they should not be restored as a troubleshooting step. Business Analysis contains metric
findings, anomalies, relationships, limitations and decision evidence together.

The advanced relationship explorer and its dedicated worker were also retired.
Historical evidence may remain readable, but that is not a new advanced-analysis
service. Use the multi dataset report for current multi-metric analysis.

## Which analysis tool should I use?

Start with the question you need to answer, and choose a tool your records can
support. For example, "Which ads earned more per dollar last month?" is about
past results. "How should I split next month's budget?" is about what to test next.

In Business Analysis, select one of these tabs. On smaller screens, choose it
from the Analysis tool dropdown.

- [Compare metrics](/business-analysis): "What changed, and did other numbers
  move with it?" Use dates and metrics such as ad spend and orders. Choose
  Multi-dataset report for two or more sources, or [Single dataset report](/business-analysis/dataset)
  for one. Preview analysis shows totals, trends and unusual changes where
  supported. Optional correlation shows whether metrics moved together and
  whether one tended to move first. It doesn't show which ad earned a sale.
- [Business impact](/business-analysis/impact): "Did the change I made help?"
  For example, compare orders at a store that extended its hours with
  orders at a similar store that kept its hours. You need an eligible saved
  decision, its actual start date, an unaffected comparison and enough matching
  history before and after. Analyze business impact estimates what changed
  beyond the comparison, if the evidence supports it. It does not prove cause.
- [Return on spend](/business-analysis/returns): "Which channel earned more
  revenue per dollar I already spent?" Choose matched spending and recorded
  sales by channel for the same dates and currency. Compare results divides
  revenue by spending: $300 on $100 of spend is $3 per dollar. Count each sale
  under one channel using the same rule for all channels. With only total store
  sales, you can't fairly rank channels. This measures revenue, not profit.
- [Profitability](/business-analysis/profitability): "Which product, service
  or location leaves the most money after costs?" Select recorded revenue and
  the total costs for those same sales. Compare results shows money left and
  margin. A best seller can leave less money after fees, delivery or product
  costs. The result isn't net profit unless you've included all relevant costs.
- [Customer retention](/business-analysis/retention): "Do buyers come back,
  and how soon?" Use completed purchases with dates, a customer ID and a
  purchase ID. Choose 30, 60 or 90 days, then Show results to see how many
  eligible buyers made a second purchase. Buyers without enough follow-up
  time are too early to judge. Use the result to pick a reminder timing to test.
- [Sales conversion](/business-analysis/conversion): "Where do I lose potential
  sales?" Use counts for the same people or opportunities at each step, such
  as 100 enquiries, 70 quotes and 20 sales. Show results displays conversion
  rates and drop-offs. Here, 50 quotes didn't become sales, so that's a place
  to investigate. Unrelated website clicks and shop orders aren't a sales funnel.
- [Cash flow planning](/business-analysis/cash-flow): "Can I pay my bills if a
  customer pays late?" Enter the cash available at the start and use schedules
  of expected receipts and payments, from one or two datasets. Preview cash plan
  shows weekly balances and the lowest point. Try a receipt delay to see when
  cash might fall short even if the business is profitable. It does not verify
  your bank balance or move money.

Use these separate pages when you want to plan ahead or check the same numbers
regularly:

- [Budget Planner](/budget-planner): "How should I split a future budget?"
  Choose a result such as orders and at least two spending options. With at
  least 104 complete matching weeks and enough evidence, it suggests a mix to
  test at the same total budget and estimates the possible difference. It does
  not rate past ad returns or change budgets for you.
- [Forecasts](/forecasts): "What might monthly orders look like next?" Choose
  a metric with usable dated history and the right grouping. The projection
  continues patterns in that history. Check the warnings, particularly if the
  business has changed. To test a proposed payment delay instead, use Cash flow planning.
- [Weekly Reports](/weekly-reports): "What needs my attention every week?"
  Choose the metrics, date columns and optional targets once. Preview and save
  to monitor last week's totals, missed targets and unusual changes where the
  data supports them. It's a recurring review, not a forecast. Email delivery
  needs usable data from at least two selected datasets in the completed week.
- [Relationships](/relationships): "I want to revisit the same pair of numbers."
  Choose two dated metrics from different datasets, such as ad spend and orders.
  Preview and save their relationship. It gives a coefficient and observed delay
  when there is enough matching data. Use Compare metrics instead to review
  several pairs, totals and unusual changes in one report.

## Find links and actions inside pages

- [Scheduled email](/alerts) is inside Weekly Reports. Choose it after saving a
  report to set delivery, add recipients, send a test and view delivery history.
  Email workspace owner in the report preview sends the report once instead.
- In [Business Analysis](/business-analysis), use Build analysis and Analysis
  preview for Compare metrics. Choose Saved reports to reopen evidence. In
  Business impact, use Assessment setup, Assessment preview or Saved assessments.
  The five other Business Analysis tools use Saved comparisons for their own results.
- In the [Dashboard](/dashboard), use Compare data to join dated summaries.
  Use Review joined data to inspect or undo the join. Use Share link to enter
  recipients, or Manage shared links to stop a single link. Save PDF exports
  the view where available. Dashboard sharing does not invite a workspace member.
- In [Datasets](/datasets), Add data offers Upload file, Import link or
  Connection where available. Use a dataset row to open its preview and metric
  selection. In [Connections](/connections), Configure added connections is
  visible only after adding a source. Use Advanced date range for an older pull.
- In [Settings](/settings), Workspace Members invites teammates. An agency uses
  Client Workspaces to Create and invite owner or Link an existing workspace.
  A client owner uses Agency connection or Share workspace when available.
  Eligible owners edit and publish their branding under Hosted portal settings.
  Use Delete workspace only when you intend to remove that workspace permanently.
- In [Decisions](/decisions), open a decision to review or record its result.
  Assess business impact opens that decision in Business Analysis when supported.
  Continue decision reopens an unfinished decision instead of creating another.
- In [Help & Support](/help), use Browse all tool guides to read steps by task.
  Use Contact Support, Report a Bug or Request a Feature to open the relevant form.
- The account menu lets you sign out. Use the workspace selector to switch
  workspaces when you have access to more than one.
- [Privacy](/privacy) describes how data is handled. [Security](/security)
  summarizes safeguards. [Terms](/terms) describes the service terms. These
  pages do not change workspace preferences or subscriptions.

## What can Ask Decisionate do?

Ask Decisionate on Help & Support explains approved product guidance and provides
source sections. It does not read your live datasets, rank your own channels,
inspect your account, run analyses or change settings. Use the relevant tool in
your workspace for your results. A guidance answer is not an account investigation.
If it cannot match your question to a reviewed answer, it says it cannot
confirm it. Related questions are suggestions, not answers. Use Contact support
for account-specific help, without sending secrets or private records.

## What a recommendation means

A recommendation is evidence for human review, not an executed decision.
Decisionate does not automatically increase advertising budgets, alter a source
system, or guarantee business improvement when an analysis finishes.
