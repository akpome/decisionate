# Weekly email format, sender and branding

The weekly report is an HTML email with a plain-text alternative. It is not a
PDF attachment and currently does not embed the report preview's indexed trend
chart.

## What appears in the HTML email

The header identifies the workspace and branding. The cross-dataset review
includes the business question, current and previous windows, dimension filters,
observations, selected relationship interpretations, warnings and any linked
decisions. A separate unusual-changes section gives per-metric anomaly results
and history limitations.

The KPI summary shows selected metrics and datasets, current/previous values,
units, percentage change when valid, and configured targets. The recommendations
area can include an AI or fallback summary. A Review report button opens the
application, followed by prepared-for/by information and Powered by Decisionate.

The plain-text alternative also includes analysis-source/confidence information
and supported risks/relationship context. Do not claim that the current HTML
template displays every field of the plain-text alternative or full app preview.

## Which sender address clients see

Client weekly reports use the platform email sender configured by an authorized
platform administrator, with an agency display name such as Agency Name via
Decisionate. They do not automatically send from the agency's domain.

No specific address such as no-reply or alert is hardcoded as the weekly sender.
Confirm the current platform sender setting or a relevant real delivery before
claiming the exact address. A previous screenshot or older system email proves
only that historical observation. Marketing sender details are separate.

## Logos

Business and agency-workspace owners receive Decisionate branding. Agency-managed
clients receive the managing agency's configured logo and colors when available.
The agency logo does not change the verified sending domain. The current weekly
message builder does not automatically set an agency Reply-To address.
