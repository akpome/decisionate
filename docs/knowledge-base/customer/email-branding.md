# Weekly email format, sender and branding

The weekly report is an HTML email with a plain-text alternative. It is not a
PDF attachment and currently does not embed the report preview's indexed trend
chart.

## Set up scheduled weekly email

1. In Weekly Reports, preview and save the weekly report. A new saved setup
   enables owner delivery by default when the report has sufficient data.
   You do not need to add the workspace owner's email again.
2. Open Scheduled email from Weekly Reports. This is the page older links
   may call Alerts; you do not need to find a separate sidebar tab. To add
   recipients, enter Additional email addresses and
   choose the Delivery day. The owner is always included.
3. Use Send automatically each week to enable or pause automatic delivery,
   then choose Save email settings. This does not change what the report
   measures. Edit report metrics or the reporting timezone in Weekly Reports.
4. Choose Send test email after saving to check delivery. The test does not
   require report data and has no results. Review Delivery history before
   retrying a send with an uncertain status.

To send the actual report once to its owner, use Email workspace owner on
Weekly Reports. It requires usable data for the report week and is separate
from both the recurring schedule and the test. Opening Scheduled email does
not send anything.

## What appears in the HTML email

The current Weekly Intelligence email includes a weekly summary, up to three
priority findings and their recommended investigations, relevant decisions and
outcomes, and key data limitations. When dimension filters are selected, a
Selected data section lists each dataset, column and selected values in both
HTML and plain text. Those results describe the filtered records, not the whole
business.

The subject identifies the workspace and completed report week. Read the full
report opens the application for the detailed metrics, anomaly results and
relationship evidence. The footer includes prepared-for/by information and
platform branding. The email is a concise summary, not every field or chart
from the full report.

Older report formats can contain the expanded KPI summary, current and previous
windows, targets, relationship interpretations, anomaly results and AI or
rules-based analysis. Do not assume every older email section appears in the
current Weekly Intelligence summary.

## Which sender address clients see

Client weekly reports use the platform email sender configured by an authorized
platform administrator, with an agency display name such as Agency Name via
Decisionate. They do not automatically send from the agency's domain.

No specific address such as no-reply or alert is hardcoded as the weekly sender.
Confirm the current platform sender setting or a relevant real delivery before
claiming the exact address. A previous screenshot or older system email proves
only that historical observation. Marketing sender details are separate.

## Logos

Business and agency-workspace owners receive Decisionate branding. Agency-managed
clients receive the managing agency's configured logo and colors when available.
The agency logo does not change the verified sending domain. The current weekly
message builder does not automatically set an agency Reply-To address.
