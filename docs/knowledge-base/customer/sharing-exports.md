# Share dashboards and retain evidence

Sharing a dashboard, saving a dashboard PDF, exporting decisions and saving a
report snapshot are separate operations. Choose the one that matches the audience
and whether you need a live view or frozen evidence.

## Public dashboard links

Use authorized sharing controls only after checking the workspace, dataset,
metrics, mappings and filters. A shared dashboard can be opened by people who
have its link under the current sharing policy. Do not include sensitive segments
or assume a public link has the same boundary as an authenticated workspace page.

Choose Share link to open the email dialog. Add up to ten email addresses,
separated by commas or new lines, then choose Send email. Opening the dialog or
canceling it does not send anything. Sending saves the selected dashboard view
and emails a read-only link to each recipient separately; their addresses are
not disclosed to other recipients. Existing dashboard sharing controls remain
available. No email application needs to be installed on your computer.

Stop sharing when the audience or intended purpose changes. If a shared view
looks different, compare the dataset, selected metrics, time window, grouping,
mapping and dimension filter before attributing the difference to the source.
Refresh or republish only through the application's supported controls.

## Email a business analysis report

In Business Analysis, preview a multi-dataset report or a single-dataset report,
then choose Share link. Enter the recipients and choose Send email. If the preview
has not been saved, the dialog explains that a saved copy will be shared.
Recipients see the saved metric totals, chart, included relationship findings,
anomaly checks and interpretation. The chart can switch between indexed and
actual values. Opening the email does not recalculate the analysis. Private
decision records, drafts, workspace settings and editing controls are not shared.
This control is separate from scheduled weekly emails and business-impact assessments.

Anyone holding the emailed link can view the shared results without signing in;
an email address is a delivery destination, not an access restriction. Forwarding
the email also forwards access. Choose Stop sharing on the original report to
revoke all its links. Sharing it again creates a new link; old revoked links
remain unavailable. Deleting the report or a source also removes access.

Send confirmation means the email provider accepted the message, not that it
reached an inbox. Check spam folders. A partial or uncertain send states how many
recipients were confirmed; check other recipients' inboxes before sending again.
The app does not automatically repeat an uncertain send. A workspace may submit
up to ten sharing email batches per ten minutes. Workspace access and the
application's centrally configured email provider are still required to send.

## PDFs and exports

Save PDF captures the supported dashboard view; verify its visible period and
selection before distributing it. Decision exports contain decision records,
not a complete copy of every raw dataset. Available export and deletion actions
depend on the authenticated role.

A saved report snapshot preserves its generated analysis and evidence, rather
than becoming a live chart that silently changes after every sync. Recalculate
and save a new snapshot for a new period or selection.

## Weekly email is not the same export

Weekly delivery builds a completed-week review from the selected template. It
does not email the dashboard's PDF or embed the app's indexed chart in the
current template. Confirm recipients and filters separately from public sharing;
turning off a shared link does not itself change weekly email recipients.
