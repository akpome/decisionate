# Data processing, retention and email preferences

Use the current Privacy, Terms and Security pages for approved legal and security
statements. This guidance explains product distinctions; it is not legal advice
or a guarantee about every infrastructure or provider policy.

## Workspace evidence and AI

Data and analytical context must remain within the authenticated workspace's
authorization. Report AI interpretation uses bounded selected evidence, not a
deliberate upload of the full raw dataset. Some facts or recorded learning notes
can still contain sensitive information, so minimize personal content in datasets
and support requests. An AI payload shape alone does not establish a provider's
training, retention, residency or contractual behavior.

Ask Decisionate sends product questions, bounded recent user messages and
relevant customer documentation to the configured AI provider when available.
It does not load your workspace datasets or retain a server-side conversation
history. Known credential patterns are blocked and email addresses are removed
from provider-bound question text, but this is not comprehensive personal-data
redaction. Do not submit sensitive records. Provider processing and retention
terms still apply. A separate support request is sent only after you review and
submit its form.

## Retention distinctions

Connector imports use rolling three-year retention. Model lookback is a separate
limit on the history used for calculation. Uploaded files, saved evidence,
subscription expiry, exports and backups have separate policies and lifecycle
controls. A removed source row cannot be reconstructed from a historical summary
unless the necessary detail was retained elsewhere.

## Marketing versus service email

Product updates, tips and offers use a separate marketing-consent preference.
The signup marketing choice is optional and unchecked by default. Declining it
does not itself disable account access. Use the provided preferences or
unsubscribe control to change marketing participation.

Operational messages such as account/billing notices and configured weekly
workspace reports are not the same setting as marketing subscriptions. Do not
promise that unsubscribing from marketing cancels all required service notices
or disables a workspace's weekly delivery schedule.

## Requests and assurance

Use authorized support for an export, deletion or privacy request that is not
covered by self-service controls. Do not send credentials or unnecessary customer
records to prove an issue. This knowledge repository does not certify a security
audit, compliance status or backup-recovery capability.
